Background

Practitioner. Researcher. Builder.

In 2013, I walked into the office of the Mayor of Kumasi and told him his revenue collectors were stealing from the city — and that I could stop them with software.

Within three months of deploying the Business and Property Rate Management System, KMA revenue increased by 60%. The Mayor issued a formal commendation and began introducing me to district officials across Ghana.

Over the next decade, I built and maintained eleven production systems across Ghana's core public institutions. These systems serve millions of Ghanaians every day.

I am now applying for a PhD in Computer Science to develop research methodology for AI-driven decision support in civic systems.
